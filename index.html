Pitch Notes

A collection of fantasy stats by @timbayer93

I started playing Fantasy Premier League (FPL) in 2011. I quickly got interested in the data behind football and FPL. I started in Excel with various fixture tickers, moved to Tableau then Pyhton and most recently built this site. Here some of the highlights, hope you enjoy and good luck!
